# Bundled webfonts

The `.woff2` files in this directory are third-party typefaces redistributed as
part of this application. They are **not** covered by the repository's LICENSE;
each remains under its own foundry's terms.

| File(s) | Typeface | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `bespokeslab-300/400/500.woff2` | Bespoke Slab | Indian Type Foundry, via Fontshare — https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/bespoke-slab |
| `generalsans-400/500.woff2` | General Sans | Indian Type Foundry, via Fontshare — https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/general-sans |
| `redaction-50-latin-400-normal.woff2` | Redaction 50 | Titus Kaphar & Forest Young — https://www.redaction.us |

All three are distributed by their creators free of charge, including for
commercial use. The Fontshare families are issued under the ITF Free Font
Licence; Redaction is published under the SIL Open Font License.

**Before relying on this commercially, read the current licence text at the
source links above rather than this summary.** Font licensing changes, these
terms were not drafted here, and the note is a pointer — not legal advice, and
not a substitute for the foundry's own document.

The files here are subsetted for web delivery (see `scripts/subset-fonts.sh`),
which is a modification. Both licences permit subsetting for embedding; neither
permits redistributing the fonts as a standalone font product.
